Privacy Policy

Kasera · last updated 11 August 2026

Kasera stores everything on your phone. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we never receive your data. If you turn on backup, your records go to your own Google Drive — not to us.

What Kasera stores on your device

You enter this; the app keeps it in a database on your phone:

  • Properties and units, and the rent for each
  • Tenant names, mobile numbers, and emergency contacts you choose to record
  • Lease dates, deposits and advances
  • Meter readings and the photos you take of meters
  • Bills, charges, statements and payment history
  • An app-lock PIN, stored only as a cryptographic hash that cannot be reversed

None of this is transmitted to R3AL. We have no way to see it.

Information about other people

Most of what you record is about your tenants, not you. You decide what to enter and you are responsible for handling it lawfully — under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), that generally makes you the personal information controller for your tenants' details, and Kasera the tool you use.

Practical version: only record what you need, and keep your phone locked.

Google Drive backup (optional, off by default)

If you switch backup on:

  • Kasera asks Google for permission using the drive.file scope, which grants access only to files this app creates. It cannot read, list or modify anything else in your Drive, including files you made yourself.
  • Your records are uploaded to a folder in your own Google Drive as spreadsheets and, if you choose, a copy of the database file.
  • The data goes from your phone to Google. It does not pass through us.
  • Transfers use HTTPS.
  • Disconnecting stops future uploads. Backups already in your Drive stay there, under your control, and you can delete them like any other file.

Google's handling of files in your Drive is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

What Kasera does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No crash or usage reporting
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No selling or sharing of any information
  • No account creation

The only network requests the app makes are to Google Drive, and only when you have enabled backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Kasera deletes everything it stored on your phone. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Backups in your Google Drive are yours; delete them from Drive, or use Settings → Backup → Disconnect first to stop new ones.

Children

Kasera is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a notice will appear in the app listing.
